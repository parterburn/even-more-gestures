## Product description

Even More Gestures gives your trackpad a few useful new moves: rotate to switch tabs, pinch to close, spread to open or undo, and swipe with four fingers to reveal sidebars.

It begins with sensible defaults for familiar Mac apps, including Maps, Chrome, and ChatGPT. You can refine each app at any time, and your own shortcuts always take priority.

It lives in the menu bar, never in the Dock. One Accessibility permission is required because the app activates the commands already available in the app you are using.

Pay what feels fair. The download is the complete app either way.

## Download notes

- Apple silicon and Intel builds are planned for release validation; this first release is built on Apple silicon
- Download the latest notarized ZIP from GitHub Releases
- Move the app to Applications, open it, and follow the one-time Accessibility setup
